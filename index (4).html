Biggy Turns 40: A Retrospective on Cars I Cannot Afford

In which a lifelong car obsession, one very specific link, and a go-kart with a grudge all collide on the same birthday.

By Your Sibling · September 13, 2026 · 3 min read

Everyone in this family has known for roughly two decades that Biggy loves cars more than most people love their own children. It's not news. It's practically a personality trait at this point, filed somewhere between "always missing in action" and "will absolutely tell you the horsepower of a car you didn't ask about." So when, this year, exactly one link showed up on my WhatsApp, nobody needed a translator.

That's the thing about a real car guy — he only has to point at something once. No repeated hinting, no strategically-placed screenshots at 2am. One link, sent in passing, and it lodges itself permanently into the recipient's brain, where it now lives rent-free next to "his birthday is coming up" and "I have not budgeted for this."

Meanwhile: proof of commitment

As if the universe needed further evidence that Biggy takes cars seriously, he recently went go-kart racing — on Jeff's birthday, no less — and came back with the kind of injuries usually reserved for people who've made poor life choices at high speed. Most people just save pictures of cars. Biggy went and broke actual bones for the cause, on someone else's special day.

INCIDENT REPORT — FILED FOR THE RECORD
  • Vehicle: one (1) go-kart, top speed generously described as "not that fast"
  • Occasion: Jeff's birthday, now permanently overshadowed
  • Driver: Biggy, lifelong car enthusiast, first-time crash test dummy
  • Damage to the body: broken bones
  • Damage to the enthusiasm: none whatsoever
  • Official statement from driver: reportedly still worth it

Nobody asked him to prove his dedication. He did it anyway, at full send, at forty years old, on a day that belonged to someone else entirely. Please heal up so you can go back to lecturing me about horsepower at full volume.

Which brings us to the gift

I heard the hint. I looked at the actual price. I checked my account balance twice, hoping it had changed since the first time. It had not. So, in the spirit of showing up anyway…

Not to scale with your actual expectations. Batteries, fame, and horsepower not included.

The paperwork

Nothing says "I love you" quite like a legally non-binding IOU, so here's the fine print, for the record.

  • A surprise promotion nobody saw coming, including my boss
  • You personally sponsoring 60% of the purchase, no judgment
  • The toy car version mysteriously appreciating in value

Closing thoughts

40

Biggy, officially in his forties, balloons non-negotiable.

Joking aside — forty looks good on you, broken bones and all. You deserve the real car, the loud one, the one that turns heads on the way to nowhere in particular. Until then, you've got a toy car, an incident report, a ridiculous certificate, and someone who would empty their account for you the second it's not empty already.

Happy 40th, Biggy. I love you, big guy.
